# Refund and Returns Policy
Version: 1.0

## Return window
Eligible unused products may be returned within 14 calendar days after delivery. Products must be complete and include original packaging. Opened products are reviewed by support and are not automatically eligible for a refund.

## Shipping and processing
If Pearlz shipped the wrong item or the item arrived damaged, Pearlz arranges return shipping after the customer provides photographs. For a change-of-mind return, the customer pays return shipping. Approved refunds are generally processed within 5–10 business days after inspection; bank posting time may vary.

## Separate processes
A return is not the same as a warranty repair or replacement. Promotional bundles may be partially refunded only when support confirms the remaining item values. This policy does not specify student discounts or instalment payment options.

## Return eligibility and timing
Eligible unused products may be returned within 14 calendar days after delivery. The item must be complete and include original packaging. Opened products are reviewed by support and are not automatically eligible for a refund. The 14-day return window is not the same as a product warranty period. A fault reported outside the return window may still require warranty triage, but it must not be labelled an automatically approved return.

## Shipping cost and inspection
If Pearlz shipped the wrong item or the item arrived damaged, Pearlz arranges return shipping after the customer provides photographs. For a change-of-mind return, the customer pays return shipping. Approved refunds are generally processed within 5–10 business days after inspection; bank posting time may vary. The processing clock begins after inspection and approval, not when the customer first sends a message. Agents should explain this distinction without promising an exact bank posting date.

## Condition and partial refunds
The returned product should include the main unit, supplied accessories, documents, and original packaging. Missing parts or signs of use require a support review and may affect eligibility. This policy does not define a universal restocking percentage, so agents must not invent one. Promotional bundles may be partially refunded only when support confirms the remaining item values. Do not calculate a bundle refund by simply dividing the bundle price evenly among items unless the approved order record supplies those values.

## Three distinct service routes
A return is a request to send back an eligible purchase. A replacement is a request to receive another item, often after an incorrect or damaged delivery or an approved fault review. A warranty repair is a technical service route governed by the warranty policy. These processes may share evidence, but one does not automatically guarantee the outcome of another. Ask what outcome the customer is requesting and route the case to the correct process.

## Missing policy details and safe wording
This policy does not specify student discounts, instalment payments, universal restocking fees, or a blanket refund for consequential property damage. If asked, state that the available documentation does not define the answer and offer a human support review. Do not approve refunds, alter orders, create shipping labels, or claim that a payment has been returned; the assistant has no transaction authority. A final decision must be made by an authorised support agent after the order and condition have been checked.
