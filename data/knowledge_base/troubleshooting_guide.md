# Troubleshooting Guide
Version: 1.0

## Three beeps or red blinking light
On AquaPearl 500 Pro, three short beeps with a red indicator may mean the sediment pre-filter is saturated (alert E-03). Turn the unit off before replacing the pre-filter. After replacement, hold RESET for five seconds. If the alert remains, stop and contact support.

## Low flow
Check whether the filter replacement interval has elapsed. Do not open the pump housing. If flow remains low after the documented filter check, contact service.

## Leaking base
Turn off power and water supply immediately. Do not continue operating the unit or open the electrical compartment. Photograph the leak and contact support for safe diagnosis.

## High TDS reading
Re-check the reading using the normal sampling procedure in the product manual. The knowledge base does not define certification status from a TDS reading alone.

## Safety
Customers must not open sealed electrical or pump components. Stop using the device if there is burning smell, exposed wiring, or water near electrical connections.


## Additional symptom checks

| Symptom | Possible explanation | Safe customer action | Escalation boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| No power indicator | Outlet, adapter connection or unit fault | Check the adapter is connected to a safe, working outlet without touching wet parts | Damaged cable, hot adapter or repeated trips: stop use |
| Low water flow | Loaded filter, kinked tubing or supply restriction | Inspect visible tubing and supported filter indicator | Do not open housing or increase pressure |
| Water under the base | Loose external connection or internal leak | Close inlet valve and keep power disconnected | Any water near electrical parts: urgent human support |
| Persistent unusual taste | Flush incomplete or unresolved water issue | Complete documented flush only once as instructed | If persistent, stop drinking and contact support |
| Continuous running sound | Normal cycle or flow-control issue | Check whether the sound stops after the documented cycle | Persistent or unfamiliar sound: service review |
| Three beeps / E-03 | Sediment pre-filter saturation | Replace compatible pre-filter and hold RESET five seconds | If alert persists, contact support |
| Red light not defined in manual | Unknown alert or model/firmware variation | Record the exact pattern and model | Do not map it to E-03 without evidence |
| High TDS reading | Input-water variation or measurement uncertainty | Record reading and conditions | The knowledge base gives no universal threshold; escalate |
| Pump unusually noisy | Air, restriction or mechanical fault | Check visible tubing for kinks only | Do not open the unit; request service |
| App will not pair | Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or permission issue | Check app permissions and follow the app's normal pairing flow | Do not share account passwords or one-time codes |
| Filter reminder remains after replacement | Cartridge not seated or reset incomplete | Verify compatibility and follow manual reset instructions | Persistent reminder: support |
| Adapter feels hot | Electrical or load issue | Stop using and disconnect only if safe | Burning smell or hot cable: urgent escalation |
| Repeated breaker trip | Electrical fault or unsuitable circuit | Stop operation | Do not repeatedly reset breaker; qualified support |
| Water is cloudy immediately after filter change | Trapped air or carbon fines | Follow initial flush procedure | If persistent, stop drinking and contact support |
| Tubing appears cracked | Age, impact or poor fit | Close water inlet and keep power disconnected | Do not patch with tape; service review |
| Unit was dropped | Internal or external damage possible | Do not operate until inspected | Photograph damage and contact support |
| Customer reports burning smell | Possible electrical overheating | Stop use and keep away from unit | Urgent human escalation; no troubleshooting beyond safe isolation |
| Water near electrical plug | Immediate electrical hazard | Do not touch wet plug or water; isolate supply only if safe | Emergency/safety escalation; qualified help required |

## General troubleshooting policy
The table is a triage aid, not a repair manual. It does not authorise removal of covers, internal wiring checks, bypassing sensors, changing the adapter, or use of unapproved chemicals. If the symptom is not described, the correct action is to record it and ask support rather than select the closest-looking code. Safety-sensitive symptoms take priority over normal service and warranty questions.
