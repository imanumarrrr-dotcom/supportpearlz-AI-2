# Service and Maintenance Agreement
Version: 1.1 | Last updated: 2026-02-15

## Claim procedure
To start a warranty claim, provide the original invoice, serial number, a description of the fault, and photographs or a short video when relevant. Support creates a case number and advises whether remote diagnosis or a service visit is required. Do not ship the unit before receiving case instructions. Support may ask for model, purchase date, current indicator state, cartridge installation date, and a safe description of the installation environment. Do not ask the customer to open the unit to gather evidence.

## Service plan overview
The service desk can record interest in maintenance support, but the current starter documentation does not define published prices or guaranteed response-time SLAs for named plan tiers. Therefore, an agent must confirm the current plan terms before quoting a fee or arrival time. A service plan does not automatically expand the standard product warranty unless the written plan explicitly says so.

## Preventive visit scope
A qualified technician may inspect external tubing, fittings, visible seals, cartridge seating, and documented indicator states. Any internal electrical or pump work must follow approved service procedures. The technician should record findings, parts replaced, customer approval for billable work, and the case number. If a part is outside warranty coverage, it is billed separately only after a quote is approved.

## Parts and consumables
Replacement filters and other consumables are excluded from the standard limited warranty. A maintenance plan may have separate consumable terms, but those terms must be checked in the active agreement. Do not tell a customer that all filters, tubing, or accessories are free under every plan. Where the available document does not specify a part's coverage, state that a service agent must confirm it.

## Cancellation and service appointment
The starter documentation does not define a universal cancellation fee, pro-rata refund formula, or exact visit price. These values must be confirmed from the customer's active plan and current booking record. If an appointment needs to be moved, support should check technician availability before confirming a new slot. The assistant must not claim an appointment has been booked or cancelled unless a connected booking system confirms the action.

## Safety escalation
Leaks near electrical components, burning smells, exposed wiring, damaged adapters, or repeated electrical trips must be treated as safety-sensitive. Stop use and escalate to human support rather than attempting repairs. A service visit is not a reason to continue operating an unsafe device while waiting.
