# AeroPearl Mini Product Manual
Version: 1.0 | Last updated: 2026-02-01

## Product purpose and contents
AeroPearl Mini is a compact indoor air purifier. The standard package contains one AeroPearl Mini unit, a pre-installed starter filter, a power adapter, a quick-start sheet and a warranty card. Any optional stand or mounting part is included only if shown on the order confirmation. Its limited warranty lasts 12 months from the invoice date.

## Placement and room use
Place the unit upright on a firm, level surface with unobstructed air intake and outlet areas. Do not place it inside a closed cabinet, against curtains, beside a heat source, or where water can splash onto it. Keep the intake clear of dust accumulation and do not cover the outlet to reduce noise. Room performance depends on room size, airflow and filter condition; the available documentation does not provide a guaranteed air-change rate for every room.

## Controls and filter service
Use the power control and the speed setting documented on the unit. A filter reminder indicates that maintenance should be checked; it is not a measurement of all airborne contaminants. Replace the filter with a part explicitly marked compatible with AeroPearl Mini. Disconnect power before opening the filter cover. Do not wash a disposable filter or reinstall it while damp. After replacement, close the cover fully and reset the reminder only as described by the quick-start instructions.

## Cleaning and safe operation
Clean the outer housing with a soft dry or slightly damp cloth after disconnecting power. Do not spray aerosols into the intake, use solvents, or open the motor compartment. Stop use if the adapter becomes unusually hot, the cable is damaged, there is a burning smell, or the unit makes an unfamiliar grinding noise. Do not insert tools or fingers into the fan area. Contact support and provide the serial number and a description of the observed indicator pattern.

## Troubleshooting boundaries
If airflow seems weak, first confirm that intake and outlet are unobstructed and that the filter is correctly installed. If noise changes after the unit is moved, check that it is on a level surface and not touching another object. Persistent noise, repeated shutdowns, or a burning smell require service assessment. The manual does not document a customer-accessible motor repair. Do not disassemble the unit to diagnose a suspected motor fault.

## Warranty and records
Retain the invoice and warranty card. The warranty period starts on the invoice date, not the delivery date. Consumable filters, physical damage, unauthorised repair, commercial use and damage caused by unsuitable conditions are excluded under the general warranty policy. A customer should contact support before shipping the product; the support team will provide a case number and next steps.
