# Privacy Policy
Version: 1.0

## Data collected
For an order, Pearlz may collect name, delivery address, email, phone number, order details, and payment confirmation supplied by the payment processor. The mobile app may collect device type, app version, account preferences, and water usage telemetry when the feature is enabled.

## Sharing and retention
Order details may be shared with the courier for delivery and with a payment processor to complete transactions. Support records are retained for 24 months after ticket closure unless a longer period is required for a documented dispute. This starter policy does not specify all telemetry retention periods.

## Customer rights
Customers may request access to, export of, or deletion of eligible account data by contacting human support. Some transaction records may need to be retained for accounting or dispute handling. Cookie and analytics details should be confirmed with support because they are not fully described in this starter extract.

## Connected-device telemetry details
When a customer chooses to connect a supported device to the mobile application, the app may process operational telemetry such as device status, filter reminder state, reported fault codes, connection status, and limited usage summaries needed to display the device dashboard. Telemetry is not a laboratory certificate and should not be represented as proof that water is safe for every possible contaminant. The app should collect only what is needed for the connected features the customer has enabled. A support agent investigating an issue should explain the purpose of a requested diagnostic record and avoid asking the customer to send unrelated screenshots or personal messages.

## Account and purchase records
The store processes order contact details, delivery address, order contents, invoice information, and transaction status so the purchase can be fulfilled and supported. Payment-card authentication is handled through the payment processor; support should never ask the customer to disclose a full card number, password, security code, or one-time passcode in a chat. Courier partners receive delivery details needed to transport the parcel. These operational transfers do not authorise a courier to use the information for unrelated marketing.

## Retention and access requests
Order and invoice records may be retained for business, accounting, fraud-prevention, and dispute-resolution needs. Device telemetry is retained according to the connected-service retention schedule; the starter policy does not publish a single universal retention period for every telemetry field. If asked for a specific period not stated in the applicable notice, agents should refer the request to the privacy team rather than guessing a number. Customers may request access to, export of, or deletion of eligible personal information through the published privacy-request route. Identity verification may be required to protect the account.

## Cookies and app permissions
The online store may use essential cookies for session continuity, checkout and security. Optional analytics or preference cookies should be described according to the consent choices shown to the customer. The mobile application may request permissions needed for pairing, notifications, or connected-device functions. A permission request should be explained in relation to the feature being used; customers should not be pressured to grant unrelated permissions. Disabling a permission may reduce a feature's functionality without changing the product's basic mechanical warranty.

## Privacy incident handling
If a customer reports an account takeover, unexpected disclosure, or suspected exposure of personal information, route the case to the privacy/security process promptly. Record the minimum facts needed to investigate, including the approximate time and affected account route. Do not ask the customer to paste passwords, one-time codes, or full payment details into a support ticket. Do not promise a breach conclusion or legal notification timeline before the privacy team has investigated. A request for data deletion is not the same as an immediate deletion of records that must be retained for a documented legal or transactional purpose.

## Agent response boundaries
The privacy policy explains general data practices but does not let a support assistant view, export, or erase a customer's account records by itself. An assistant may explain the documented request process, but it must not claim that a request has been submitted unless a connected system confirms it. Where a customer asks about a specific data category or retention period not covered in the current notice, state that the documentation does not provide that exact detail and refer the customer to the privacy team.
