# Product Changelog and Release Notes
Version: 1.0 | Last updated: 2026-03-01

## Firmware 2.3 — 2025-11-10
Firmware 2.3 improved start-up stability after a brief power interruption and refined the timing of the filter reminder. The release did not change the meaning of E-03: it remains the sediment pre-filter saturation alert on AquaPearl 500 Pro. Customers should follow the product manual for replacement and reset steps.

## Firmware 2.4 — 2026-02-12
Firmware 2.4 changed the display timing for E-05 on supported AquaPearl 500 Pro units. The alert may appear after the unit detects an extended inlet-flow interruption; it should not be interpreted as proof of a failed pump. Check the water supply and visible external tubing only, then contact support if the alert persists. This release note does not define E-01, E-02, E-04, E-06, E-07 or E-08. Do not extrapolate from E-05 to other codes.

## Version identification
The firmware version may be visible in the companion app's device information panel when the unit is connected. If the app is unavailable, the customer should not enter hidden service menus or attempt a firmware downgrade. Support may request the model, serial number, app version and displayed firmware version. Share only the minimum information needed for the case.

## Update safety
Do not disconnect power during an update that the app identifies as in progress. Keep the unit in a stable network environment if the update requires connectivity. If the update fails, record the visible message and contact support rather than repeating an undocumented recovery sequence. The documentation does not promise that every older device supports every firmware release.

## Known limitations
Release notes describe documented changes only. They are not a substitute for the full service manual, and they do not authorise customers to repair electronics. If a symptom is not described here, support should investigate it rather than treating the nearest alert code as equivalent. The product manual remains the primary source for ordinary operation and safety warnings.
