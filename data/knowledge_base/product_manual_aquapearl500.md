# AquaPearl 500 Pro Product Manual — Starter Extract
Version: 1.0

## Box contents
The standard box contains one AquaPearl 500 Pro unit, one sediment pre-filter, one power adapter, inlet tubing, a quick-start sheet, and a warranty card. Optional wall-mount hardware is included only when listed on the order confirmation.

## Controls
The POWER button turns the purifier on or off. The FILTER indicator shows filter status. The RESET button clears a supported filter alert after the relevant filter has been replaced.

## Maintenance
Replace the sediment pre-filter when the device indicates saturation or at the interval stated on the filter label, whichever comes first. Use only a compatible cartridge. Disconnect power and close the water supply before routine maintenance.

## Alert codes
E-03: sediment pre-filter saturation alert. Replace the pre-filter, then hold RESET for five seconds. If the alert remains, contact service. This starter extract does not define E-01, E-02, E-04, E-05, E-06, E-07 or E-08; do not infer their meanings.

## Safety
Never open the sealed electrical compartment. Do not operate the unit with a damaged cable. If water reaches electrical components, disconnect power only if safe to do so and contact service.

## Product identity and intended use
The AquaPearl 500 Pro is a point-of-use household water purifier intended for indoor domestic use with a potable municipal or otherwise suitable incoming supply. It is not a whole-house treatment system and it does not make unsafe water safe unless the documented filtration process specifically supports that claim. Do not use it for industrial, commercial food-service, or laboratory applications. The model name identifies the product family; the serial number on the rating label identifies the individual unit for service. Record the serial number before contacting support, but do not post it publicly.

## Technical specifications and operating limits
The fictional specification sheet for this assignment defines a nominal service flow of up to 1.8 litres per minute under normal inlet conditions, a 1.6 litre internal buffer reservoir, dimensions of 340 mm high by 210 mm wide by 390 mm deep, and a 24 V DC power adapter rated at 60 W. These are planning figures, not a substitute for the rating label supplied with an individual unit. Actual flow varies with inlet pressure, temperature, filter loading, and water quality. Never increase supply pressure to compensate for low flow. The device uses a sediment pre-filter followed by a carbon stage and a final polishing stage. Only cartridges marked compatible with AquaPearl 500 Pro may be installed.

## Indicators and normal behaviour
The POWER indicator is steady when the unit is operating normally. A slow blue pulse indicates standby or a normal waiting state. The FILTER indicator changes when a supported cartridge reaches its service threshold. The indicator is a maintenance reminder, not a laboratory measurement of water quality. The RESET button clears a supported alert only after the relevant maintenance has been completed. Resetting an alert without replacing the cartridge does not restore cartridge performance. If an indicator pattern is not defined in this manual or the approved changelog, do not infer its meaning; record the pattern and contact support.

## Safe setup and first use
Before connecting tubing, inspect all fittings and ensure the water shutoff valve is accessible. Keep the unit upright and allow clearance for cartridge replacement. Use a grounded outlet matching the supplied adapter. Never modify the adapter, splice its cable, or place the adapter where water can pool. After connecting the water line, inspect every fitting while the supply is opened slowly. Keep a dry paper towel beneath joints during the first inspection so a small leak is visible. If any leak appears, close the water supply and do not energise the unit until the leak has been resolved safely.

## Initial flushing procedure
After initial installation or replacement of a filter, route the outlet to a sink or suitable drain and run the documented flush cycle for ten minutes, pausing if the product display requests it. The flush removes trapped air and loose carbon fines that may cause temporary cloudiness or a mild taste difference. Do not drink water during the flush. When the flush ends, inspect the outlet water and all fittings. If taste, odour, visible particles, or abnormal flow persists beyond the documented flush, stop using the water for drinking and contact support. Do not extend flushing indefinitely as a substitute for diagnosing a persistent fault.

## Cartridge handling
Close the water inlet and disconnect power before opening a cartridge compartment. Keep replacement cartridges sealed until use, check the printed compatibility label, and avoid touching the clean outlet surfaces. A cartridge that has been dropped, cracked, stored outside its stated conditions, or left open to contamination should not be installed. Seat the cartridge fully and check that its locking feature is engaged before restoring water. If a cartridge does not seat with normal hand pressure, stop and verify the part number rather than forcing it. Record the installation date so the next service interval can be planned.

## Cleaning the exterior
Use a soft damp cloth on the exterior housing. Do not spray cleaner directly onto the controls, vents, electrical adapter, or cartridge compartment. Abrasive powders, solvent cleaners, bleach mixtures, and scouring pads can damage the finish and seals. Disconnect power before wiping around the controls. Never immerse the unit or open the sealed electrical compartment. If a spill enters the housing, isolate the water supply and arrange support assessment rather than attempting internal drying or repair.

## Alert code E-03
E-03 is documented as a sediment pre-filter saturation alert. Replace the compatible sediment pre-filter, confirm it is seated correctly, restore the water supply, and hold RESET for five seconds. If the alert remains after correct replacement, stop repeated resets and contact support with the serial number and a description of the indicator pattern. This manual extract does not define E-01, E-02, E-04, E-05, E-06, E-07, or E-08. The absence of a definition is intentional: support must verify those codes against approved service information rather than guessing.

## Storage and extended downtime
For a planned absence, follow the shut-down instructions supplied with the product and close the inlet valve if the installation permits it safely. Do not store a wet cartridge for an unspecified period or assume that a cartridge can be reused after drying. When restarting after extended downtime, inspect tubing and seals, check for stale odour, and follow the initial-flush procedure. If the unit has been exposed to freezing conditions, impact, flooding, or contamination, do not restart it until support has assessed the risk.

## Troubleshooting boundaries
Customers may perform only the external checks described in the user documentation: confirm the outlet is not obstructed, verify the adapter is firmly connected to a safe outlet, inspect visible tubing, and check whether a supported cartridge alert is present. Do not remove sealed panels, probe electrical parts, bypass a sensor, modify tubing, or use tools inside the unit. Burning smells, water near electrical parts, exposed wires, a hot adapter, or repeated breaker trips require stopping use and contacting human support. Safety concerns take priority over preserving normal service.

## Water-quality variation and limitations
Incoming water varies by location, season, plumbing condition, and source. The product documentation describes the intended domestic use but does not establish that every source water is suitable. Customers should follow local public-health advice and any model-specific restrictions. A high or changing TDS reading alone is not a complete safety assessment, and the support assistant must not claim that a specific contaminant has been removed unless a source explicitly supports that statement. If water has a chemical smell, unusual colour, or a suspected contamination event, stop drinking it and seek appropriate local guidance while contacting support about the product.

## Cartridge storage and traceability
Store sealed cartridges in a clean, dry place away from direct sunlight, strong odours, and chemicals. Keep the product label and batch information until the cartridge has been installed. Do not use a cartridge if the package is open, wet, crushed, or past a printed date. When a customer reports repeated filter alerts, ask for the cartridge SKU and installation date rather than assuming that all replacement filters are interchangeable. A compatible cartridge may still have different service life under different water conditions.

## Moving the appliance
Before moving the unit, shut off the inlet, disconnect power safely, and follow the product-specific shutdown guidance. Do not drag a connected unit by its tubing or adapter cable. Protect exposed fittings from dirt and impact. After relocation, inspect tubing and seals and complete the documented restart checks before normal use. If the appliance was dropped or exposed to freezing temperatures, flooding, or a strong impact, do not operate it until support has reviewed the situation.

## Accessibility and household use
Keep the controls and shutoff valve reachable by the person responsible for routine operation. Route tubing so it does not create a trip hazard or interfere with cabinet doors. Children should not be allowed to pull tubing, play with the adapter, or open a cartridge compartment. A customer who cannot safely reach a wall-mounted unit should request an installation review rather than using a chair or unstable surface to service it. These precautions reduce avoidable damage and make future maintenance easier.

## What the status indicators cannot tell you
An indicator reports only the condition defined by its product documentation or firmware release note. A normal indicator does not certify that the incoming water is safe, prove that a filter has been installed correctly, or replace scheduled maintenance. Conversely, an undefined flashing pattern does not automatically mean the product is defective. Record the model, firmware version if available, exact light pattern, and circumstances when it appeared. Support can then compare the report with approved service information.

## Preparing for a support contact
Before contacting support, gather the model name, invoice date, serial number, cartridge SKU if relevant, and a concise description of the symptom. For a leak or visible damage, photographs may help, but do not touch a wet electrical connection to take a picture. Include the time the issue began and any recent change, such as a filter replacement or relocation. Do not send passwords, payment authentication codes, or unrelated personal documents. The support team will tell you whether additional evidence is necessary.

## Environmental disposal
Used filters and packaging should be disposed of according to local waste guidance. The current dataset does not specify a take-back or recycling programme, so agents must not promise a free return label or a specific recycling destination. Customers should not dismantle a used cartridge to inspect its media. If local rules treat electronic equipment separately, follow those rules for the appliance and adapter rather than placing them in ordinary household waste without checking.

## Troubleshooting after a power interruption
After a brief outage, wait for the normal start-up sequence and check the visible indicator state. Do not repeatedly unplug and reconnect the adapter in rapid succession. If the unit fails to restart, displays an undefined alert, or behaves differently after a firmware update, record the exact state and contact support. Firmware notes may explain a known behaviour, but they do not authorise a customer to downgrade software or enter hidden service menus.

## Distinguishing a filter issue from a product fault
A filter reminder, reduced flow, and a pump fault are not interchangeable descriptions. A reminder may be a maintenance signal; reduced flow can have several external causes; a pump fault requires service assessment. The assistant should not diagnose an internal component solely from a customer phrase such as “it sounds weak.” Ask for safe, observable details and use the documented symptom table. If the evidence is inconclusive, say so and escalate.
