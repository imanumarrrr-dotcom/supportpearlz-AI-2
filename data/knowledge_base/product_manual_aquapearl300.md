# AquaPearl 300 Product Manual
Version: 1.0 | Last updated: 2026-02-01

## Purpose and package
AquaPearl 300 is a domestic water purifier for indoor use. The standard package includes one unit, one compatible starter filter set, a power adapter, inlet tubing, a quick-start sheet and a warranty card. Optional mounting accessories are included only when listed on the order confirmation. Check the model label before opening consumables. The warranty period is 18 months from invoice date, as specified in the warranty policy.

## Installation principles
Install on a stable, dry surface with a reachable water shutoff and grounded electrical outlet. Follow the pressure and clearance limits printed in the model-specific installation sheet; this overview does not provide a substitute numerical pressure range. Keep tubing free of sharp bends and do not use unapproved adapters. Inspect the inlet and outlet joints while slowly restoring water. If a leak is seen, close the supply and keep power disconnected until the issue is resolved.

## Daily operation
Use the normal power control to start or stop operation. Allow the system to complete its start-up sequence before drawing water. A change in flow can indicate a loaded filter, a kinked tube, or a supply issue; check only the external conditions described in the guide. Do not open the housing to inspect the pump or electronics. If normal flow does not return after checking external tubing and the filter indicator, contact support with the serial number and a description of the symptom.

## Filter maintenance
Use only replacement filters whose packaging explicitly lists AquaPearl 300 compatibility. Follow the interval printed on the installed cartridge and replace sooner if the device issues a supported service alert. Close the water supply and disconnect power before changing a filter. Seat the cartridge fully, inspect the seal, restore water slowly, and inspect for leaks. Complete the initial flush before drinking. The documentation does not publish a universal life expectancy for every water condition, so agents should not promise a fixed number of litres beyond the cartridge label.

## Cleaning and safety
Wipe the exterior with a soft damp cloth. Never immerse the unit, spray liquid into controls, or open the sealed electrical compartment. Do not operate with a damaged power cable, visible cracks near a water connection, or evidence of water near electrical parts. Burning smell, exposed wiring, or repeated electrical trips require stopping use and human escalation. The manual does not define every alert code; undefined patterns must be verified by support rather than guessed.

## Service records
Keep the invoice, serial number, cartridge installation date, and any support case number. These records help support determine whether a reported fault falls within the 18-month term and whether the relevant evidence is available. Do not ship the unit until support provides instructions. A service assessment may be required before a repair or replacement decision is made.
