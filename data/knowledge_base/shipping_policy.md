# Shipping Policy
Version: 1.0

## Delivery tiers
Metro locations typically receive orders in 2–3 working days. Secondary cities typically receive orders in 3–5 working days. Remote areas typically receive orders in 5–7 working days. Skardu is classified as a remote area.

## Dispatch
Orders placed before 12:00 noon on a working day are normally dispatched the same day. Next-day delivery is offered only in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad when the checkout page shows the option. The documentation does not specify paid next-day exceptions for remote areas.

## Tracking and failed delivery
A tracking link is sent after dispatch. After a failed delivery attempt, the courier may arrange another attempt; customers should contact support if tracking does not update for two working days.

## Delivery tiers and estimates
Metro locations typically receive orders in 2–3 working days. Secondary cities typically receive orders in 3–5 working days. Remote areas typically receive orders in 5–7 working days. Skardu is classified as a remote area. These are typical estimates rather than guaranteed arrival appointments. Weather, public holidays, courier capacity, address validation, and security restrictions can affect transit. Agents should distinguish working days from calendar days and should not convert an estimate into a guarantee.

## Dispatch cut-off and order handling
Orders placed before 12:00 noon on a working day are normally dispatched the same day. Orders placed after the cut-off or on non-working days are normally processed on the next working day. Dispatch means hand-off to the courier; it does not mean delivery on that day. An order may remain in processing while payment or address details are verified. If a customer asks for a guaranteed dispatch time, use the order record rather than relying only on the general policy.

## Express service and delivery coverage
Next-day delivery is offered only in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad when the checkout page shows the option. The documentation does not specify paid next-day exceptions for remote areas. Agents must not promise express delivery to another city based on a customer's urgency. International shipping availability is not defined in this policy; do not infer that domestic service implies international service. A customer who needs an exception should be referred to an agent who can inspect the live checkout options.

## Packaging and fragile equipment
Purifiers and replacement cartridges should be packed to reduce impact, moisture exposure, and movement during transport. The courier label and tracking number are associated with the order record. If a parcel arrives visibly crushed, wet, open, or punctured, the customer should photograph the external packaging and the product before discarding packing material. This evidence helps the delivery-damage review. Do not instruct customers to energise a unit that appears physically damaged or wet near electrical parts.

## Tracking and failed delivery
A tracking link is sent after dispatch. Courier tracking may not update continuously while a parcel is between facilities. After a failed delivery attempt, the courier may arrange another attempt; customers should contact support if tracking does not update for two working days. If the address is incomplete or the recipient is unavailable, support may need to verify the address and delivery instructions. Never ask a customer to share a payment password or one-time passcode to resolve a delivery issue.
