# Installation Guide
Version: 1.0

## First-run flushing
After installing a new filter, flush the unit using the initial-flush procedure in the product manual before drinking the water. A temporary taste difference after filter replacement may occur while residual air and loose carbon fines are flushed. If unusual taste persists after the documented flush, stop drinking the water and contact support.

## Before installation
Place the unit on a stable surface with access to a grounded electrical outlet and a shutoff valve. Check the model-specific manual for pressure and clearance limits before connecting the water line. Do not proceed if the supply pressure is outside the manual's range.

## Wall mounting
Use suitable wall anchors for the wall material. Do not mount above heat sources. If the customer is unsure about plumbing or electrical work, arrange professional installation through support. The exact current installation fee is not stated in this document.

## Pre-installation checklist
Place the unit on a stable surface with access to a grounded electrical outlet and a shutoff valve. Check the model-specific manual for pressure and clearance limits before connecting the water line. Do not proceed if supply pressure is outside the manual's range. Inspect the package for impact damage and confirm that the model on the label matches the order. Keep the invoice, quick-start sheet, and warranty card for future support. If the installation point is damp, exposed to heat, or difficult to isolate from water, ask a qualified installer to assess it first.

## Tools and workspace
A typical installation may require an adjustable wrench, a suitable drill and wall anchors for a wall-mounted configuration, a clean towel, a container for initial flushing, and the fittings specified for the model. The guide does not authorise improvising incompatible fittings or using sealants that are not approved for potable-water systems. Keep electrical tools away from standing water. Before drilling, verify that hidden plumbing or wiring is not in the drilling path. If the wall material or mounting load is uncertain, do not guess at anchor size.

## Under-sink arrangement
Confirm that the cabinet has enough room for the unit, tubing bends, cartridge access, and future maintenance. Keep the adapter and electrical connection above areas where leaks could pool. Route tubing without sharp kinks or tension, and avoid contact with hot pipes or moving drawers. Ensure the shutoff valve remains reachable after installation. Slowly restore the water supply and inspect all joints before connecting power. If a joint leaks, close the valve and correct the installation safely; do not tighten fittings while the system is pressurised beyond the documented procedure.

## Wall mounting
Use suitable anchors for the wall material and follow the mounting-hole dimensions in the model-specific installation sheet. Do not mount above a cooker, heater, or another heat source. The bracket must sit level and must not flex under the unit's weight. Confirm the cartridge door can open and that the tubing is not pinched when the unit is positioned. If the wall is hollow, cracked, damp, or otherwise unsuitable, seek professional advice. Do not use adhesive-only mounting for a product designed for mechanical anchoring.

## First-run flush and post-installation check
After installing a new filter, flush the unit using the initial-flush procedure in the product manual before drinking the water. A temporary taste difference after filter replacement may occur while residual air and loose carbon fines are flushed. If unusual taste persists after the documented flush, stop drinking the water and contact support. Check the outlet flow, fittings, and indicator state. Record the installation date and any alert displayed. A successful flush does not override a later leak, persistent odour, or unexplained water-quality concern.

## Professional installation and warranty boundaries
Support can record a request for professional installation, but the exact current installation fee is not stated in this document. Ask the customer to confirm the price through the current booking process before accepting a charge. Improper plumbing, use of incompatible parts, modification of the electrical adapter, or unauthorised internal repair may affect warranty eligibility. Do not claim that every self-installation voids the warranty; the written exclusion specifically concerns unauthorised repair and other listed exclusions. When unsure, route the case for human review.
