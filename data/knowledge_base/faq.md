# Frequently Asked Questions
Version: 1.1 | Last updated: 2026-03-01

## Products and setup
**What comes in the AquaPearl 500 Pro box?** One unit, a sediment pre-filter, power adapter, inlet tubing, quick-start sheet and warranty card. Optional wall hardware is included only when the order confirmation lists it.

**Can I put the purifier in a wet cabinet?** No. Keep the unit and adapter away from standing water. If water is near electrical parts, stop use and contact support.

**Do I need to flush a new filter?** Yes. Follow the initial-flush procedure in the product manual before drinking water.

**Why does water taste different after a filter change?** Residual air and loose carbon fines may cause a temporary difference during the documented flush. If the unusual taste persists after flushing, stop drinking the water and contact support.

**Can I open the electrical cover to check a noise?** No. The electrical compartment is sealed and customer repair is not authorised.

**What does three beeps and a filter light mean?** For AquaPearl 500 Pro, the documented E-03 alert means sediment pre-filter saturation. Replace the compatible filter and follow the five-second RESET instruction. If the alert persists, contact support. Do not assume every beep pattern has the same meaning.

**What do E-01 or E-08 mean?** The current customer manual extract does not define these codes. Record the pattern and ask support to verify it; do not guess.

## Filters and maintenance
**How often should I replace a filter?** Follow the interval printed on the compatible cartridge and any supported alert. Actual service life varies with water quality and usage. The knowledge base does not define a universal litre count for every household.

**Can I wash and reuse a disposable cartridge?** No such reuse procedure is documented. Use the correct compatible replacement and do not reinstall a damaged or contaminated cartridge.

**How do I clean the outside?** Disconnect power and use a soft damp cloth. Do not spray liquid into controls or open the housing.

**Why is the flow slow?** Possible external causes include a kinked tube, restricted outlet or loaded filter. Check only the safe external checks in the manual. If the issue persists, contact support rather than opening the unit.

## Warranty and claims
**How long is the AquaPearl 500 Pro warranty?** 24 months from the invoice date for the covered body, pump and electronic control board, subject to exclusions.

**Does delivery date start the warranty?** No. The invoice date starts the period.

**Are filters covered?** Replacement filters and other consumables are excluded from the standard limited warranty.

**What if a technician already opened my purifier?** Unauthorised repair is a stated exclusion. Tell support what happened and request a case review; do not assume an outcome before the facts are checked.

**Will Pearlz pay for a damaged kitchen cabinet?** The warranty policy does not promise compensation for customer property. Report the incident and ask for human review; do not assume reimbursement.

**How do I start a claim?** Provide the invoice, serial number, fault description and relevant photos or video. Support will issue a case number and tell you whether remote diagnosis or a visit is required. Do not ship the unit before receiving instructions.

**Does warranty transfer when I sell the unit?** Not automatically. Support must confirm a transfer in writing. Discontinued models depend on the unexpired term shown on the original extended-warranty certificate.

## Returns and refunds
**Can I return a product?** Eligible unused products may be returned within 14 calendar days after delivery, complete and in original packaging. Opened products need support review and are not automatically eligible.

**Who pays return shipping?** Pearlz arranges shipping for a wrong item or delivery damage after receiving photographs. Change-of-mind returns are paid by the customer.

**When will I get my refund?** Approved refunds are generally processed within 5–10 business days after inspection. Bank posting times can vary.

**Can I get a partial refund for a bundle?** Only if support confirms the values of the remaining items. The FAQ does not define a universal restocking percentage.

**Can you approve my refund right now?** The assistant cannot approve or issue refunds. An authorised support agent must review the order and condition.

## Delivery and orders
**How long does delivery take?** Metro: typically 2–3 working days; secondary cities: 3–5 working days; remote areas: 5–7 working days. Skardu is remote. These are estimates, not guarantees.

**Will my order ship today?** Orders placed before noon on a working day are normally dispatched the same day. Dispatch is not the same as delivery.

**Can I get next-day delivery?** It is offered only in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad when checkout displays the option. Do not assume it is available elsewhere.

**My tracking has not changed. What now?** Contact support if tracking has not updated for two working days.

**Can I change my address after dispatch?** It is not guaranteed. Contact support promptly so the live order and courier record can be checked.

## App and privacy
**What app information is collected?** The privacy policy describes account details and limited device telemetry used to operate and troubleshoot connected features. Exact categories and retention are defined in that policy.

**Can I request a copy or deletion of my data?** The privacy policy describes access, export and deletion request routes. Identity verification may be needed before account data is disclosed or deleted.

**Is the filter NSF-certified?** The current knowledge base does not document this certification. Support must verify any certification claim; the assistant should not infer it.

## Questions not specified in the current documents
The current knowledge base does not specify student discounts, instalment payments, or a universal installation fee. It also does not provide a blanket answer about international shipping. For these questions, the assistant must state that the information is not documented and refer the customer to support rather than inventing an answer.
