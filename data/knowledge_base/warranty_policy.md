# Warranty Policy
Version: 1.0 | Last updated: 2026-01-15

## AquaPearl 500 Pro
The AquaPearl 500 Pro purifier body, pump, and electronic control board have a 24-month limited warranty. The period begins on the invoice date, not the delivery date. The original invoice and serial number are required to submit a claim.

## AquaPearl 300 and AeroPearl Mini
AquaPearl 300 has a 18-month limited warranty. AeroPearl Mini has a 12-month limited warranty. The invoice date starts the warranty period.

## Exclusions
Replacement filters and other consumables are excluded. Physical damage, unauthorised repair, commercial use, and damage caused by unsuitable incoming water are excluded. A product being within the time period does not override an exclusion. The policy does not promise compensation for damage to customer property.

## Discontinued products
Discontinued products remain covered only for the unexpired term printed on the original extended-warranty certificate. Resale does not transfer warranty coverage unless customer support confirms the transfer in writing.

## Coverage by product line
AquaPearl 500 Pro has a 24-month limited warranty for the purifier body, pump, and electronic control board. AquaPearl 300 has an 18-month limited warranty. AeroPearl Mini has a 12-month limited warranty. Each period starts on the invoice date, not the delivery date. The invoice and serial number are required to start a claim. Warranty length does not by itself establish that a particular fault is covered; the cause, product condition, and exclusions must also be reviewed.

## Coverage assessment examples
A pump that fails during the applicable term may be eligible for assessment if the unit has been used domestically, maintained with compatible cartridges, and not opened by an unauthorised repairer. A cosmetic scratch reported after delivery is not automatically a warranty defect and should be routed through the delivery-damage process with photographs. A filter that reaches its normal service interval is a consumable replacement, not a warranty repair. A control board fault should be triaged by support; customers must not open the sealed electrical compartment to establish the cause themselves.

## Exclusions and evidence
Replacement filters and other consumables are excluded. Physical damage, unauthorised repair, commercial use, and damage caused by unsuitable incoming water are excluded. The policy does not promise compensation for damage to a customer's cabinet, floor, or other property. Where a customer reports consequential property damage, agents must record the report and escalate it for human review; they must not promise reimbursement. Evidence may include an invoice, serial number, photographs, short video, installation details, and the timeline of the fault. Collect only information necessary for the claim and follow the privacy policy.

## Invoice date, ownership and discontinued models
The invoice date controls the start of the standard warranty period. Delivery delays do not shift the start date. Warranty coverage does not automatically transfer on resale; customer support must confirm a transfer in writing before an agent describes the warranty as transferred. Discontinued models remain covered only for the unexpired term printed on the original extended-warranty certificate. If the certificate is unavailable, support must review the purchase record instead of estimating a remaining term.

## Claim hand-off
Support first creates a case number and checks the invoice, serial number, reported symptoms, and available evidence. The customer should not ship a unit until support provides instructions. A service technician may need to inspect the product before coverage can be decided. If a previous unauthorised repair is reported, record the facts without promising approval or rejection beyond the written exclusion. If a safety symptom is present, instruct the customer to stop use and escalate before routine troubleshooting.
