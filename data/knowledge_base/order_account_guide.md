# Ordering and Account Guide
Version: 1.0 | Last updated: 2026-02-05

## Order confirmation
After checkout, the customer should retain the order confirmation and invoice. The order record identifies the product SKU, item price, promotion if applicable, delivery address and selected delivery option. If the confirmation does not match the item received, contact support with the order reference and photographs of the parcel label and item. Do not post a full invoice publicly because it may contain personal information.

## Address corrections
A delivery address should be checked before placing an order. Once a parcel has been dispatched, an address change may depend on courier capabilities and is not guaranteed. Customers should contact support promptly if they notice an error. Never promise that a parcel can be redirected until the live order or courier record confirms it. A postcode, city, recipient name and safe contact route may be needed for a correction, but payment credentials are not needed.

## Account access
Customers should use the account recovery flow available on the store. Support will not ask for a password or one-time passcode. If a customer suspects account compromise, advise them to change their password through the official recovery flow and contact support to review recent order activity. The knowledge base does not specify a guaranteed account recovery time, so agents should not invent one.

## Order status definitions
Placed means the checkout request was received. Processing means the order is being prepared or a required check is pending. Dispatched means the parcel has been handed to the courier. Delivered means the carrier has marked delivery complete; if the customer cannot locate the parcel, support should check the proof-of-delivery record and the delivery address. These statuses describe workflow states and do not independently prove that a refund or warranty claim has been approved.

## Cancellation and changes
The general documents do not define a universal cancellation deadline or guarantee that an order can be changed after dispatch. A request to cancel, edit an item, or change a delivery option must be checked against the live order state by an authorised agent. The assistant may explain the general process but must not claim to have changed an order.
